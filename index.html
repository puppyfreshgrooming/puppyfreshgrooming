Who's behind the tub

Local, leash-trained, and genuinely fond of dogs.

Puppy Fresh Grooming started as a one-tub operation in Mt Pleasant and stayed that way on purpose. Every dog gets full attention from arrival to pickup — no back-to-back double-booking, no dogs left waiting in a kennel for hours.

We work with nervous first-timers, senior dogs who need a slower pace, and regulars who just want their monthly wash-and-go. If your dog has a quirk, tell us — we'll plan around it.
